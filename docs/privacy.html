Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026

In short: Skyscore has no accounts and doesn't sell your data. Your location is used only to get your forecast. Your saved cities stay on your phone. We count taps anonymously, without your location, to learn which features are useful.

What Skyscore uses

  • Your location, only if you allow it, to show the weather where you are.
  • The cities you save, stored on your phone so you can swipe between them.
  • Anonymous usage counts, such as "the app was opened" or "Plans was opened and a ride option was tapped".

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier.

How your location is used

When you allow location access, your phone's approximate position is sent with the forecast request to our weather data provider, Open-Meteo, which returns the forecast for that spot. Your phone's built-in mapping service turns the position into a place name, such as "Brooklyn". We don't store your location on our servers or send it anywhere else.

You can turn location off at any time in your phone's Settings. Skyscore then shows a default city, and you can search for any other.

Searching for a city

The text you type into city search is sent to Open-Meteo's place search to find matching cities.

Saved cities

Cities you save are kept only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which features help, Skyscore records simple events, such as app opens, Plans opens, which kind of plan was tapped and which partner it opened, and shares. Each event carries a random ID created on your phone that isn't linked to your name, account, or device identifiers. These counts never include your location or the cities you save. They are processed by PostHog, our analytics provider, and kept for up to 12 months.

Partner links

Plans can open partner apps and sites, such as Uber, Lyft, Uber Eats, DoorDash, OpenTable, Viator, Fever, and Ticketmaster. When you tap one, you leave Skyscore and that partner's own privacy policy applies. Some links tell the partner that you came from Skyscore, so that we may earn a commission if you book or buy. It doesn't change what you pay, and we don't share your location or any other personal information with partners.

Sharing a day

"Share today" creates an image on your phone and hands it to your phone's share menu. We don't receive the image or see where you send it.

Selling and advertising

We don't sell or rent personal information, and Skyscore doesn't show third-party ads.

Children

Skyscore isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn location access on or off in your phone's Settings.
  • Remove saved cities in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone.
  • Contact us with any question or request about your data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, mention it in the app.
