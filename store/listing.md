Today's weather as one number, one word, and what to wear. Plus Plans: the right next move for the weather, from coffee nearby to a ride before the rain.

**Description:**

Skyscore turns the forecast into a verdict. One glance tells you how good today is to be outside, from 1 to 10, and what to do about it.

TODAY, RATED
A big score, one word that sums it up (Crisp, Swampy, Gorgeous, Raw), and what to wear: "Jacket and umbrella." Tap the word for a one-line take on the day.

ONE HEADS-UP
At most one thing worth knowing: rain at 5 PM, a sharp drop in temperature, or the best hours to get outside.

PLANS FOR THE WEATHER
Rainy evening? Order in, catch a movie, or get a ride home dry. Perfect Saturday? A park, coffee, brunch outside. Plans suggests what's actually useful right now and opens it in Maps or the app you already use.

YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
Each hour drawn as a building in a small skyline. Taller is better. See the whole week in one row.

A SKY THAT FOLLOWS THE SUN
The screen takes on the day's mood and follows the real light where you are: dawn, golden hour, sunset, night. Quiet, slow, and off if you use Reduce Motion.

YOUR CITIES
Save the cities you care about and swipe between them.

SHARE YOUR SKY
Send today's score as a clean card in one tap.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. Your saved cities stay on your phone. Your location is used only to get your forecast.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com. Some Plans links may earn us a commission; it never changes what you pay.

## App Privacy answers ("nutrition label")

Match these to the privacy policy:

- **Location → Coarse Location:** collected? Yes, used for App Functionality. Linked to the user: No. Used for tracking: No.
  (It's sent to the weather provider to fetch the forecast.)
- **Usage Data → Product Interaction:** collected? Yes (once the PostHog key is on), used for Analytics. Linked to the user: No. Used for tracking: No.
- **Search History:** No. City searches go to the geocoder to find places and aren't stored by us. If you want to be conservative, declare it as App Functionality, not linked, not tracking.
- Everything else (contact info, identifiers, purchases, etc.): Not collected.
